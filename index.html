Official Privacy Policy

NERI – Legal Case Management

Complete transparency and privacy for legal practitioners, advocates, and chambers.

Application ID: com.nyayaflow.neri • Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Zero Data Collection Commitment

NERI does not collect, record, track, or share any personal information. We do not collect personal identification details, and we never sell, transmit, or disclose any user or client records to any third party or commercial tracking network.

1. Overview and Purpose

NERI – Legal Case Management System ("the Application", "we", "our") is designed specifically for legal professionals, advocates, law firms, and legal chambers to manage case dossiers, procedural workflows, judicial hearing dates, and drafting notes.

We hold advocate-client confidentiality and judicial privilege in the highest regard. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict privacy practices, explaining why and how the application operates without storing or sharing your data with external third parties.

2. Information Collection and Storage

No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or government identification documents.

Local & Private Infrastructure:

  • On-Device Storage: Any configuration settings (such as custom server endpoints, preferences, or cached offline records) are saved strictly inside the application's sandboxed local private storage on your device.
  • Direct Client-to-Chamber Architecture: When connected to your legal chamber's backend, data is communicated strictly between your mobile device and your chosen private legal server over encrypted HTTPS. No intermediate servers or developer telemetry hubs intercept this transmission.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: The application does not embed third-party analytics trackers, advertising identifiers, behavioral monitors, or crash telemetry collection SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, or AdMob).

3. Android Permissions & How They Are Used

NERI requests only the minimal system permissions essential for its core functionality:

Permission Purpose Data Shared?
android.permission.INTERNET Enables direct synchronization between the app and your chamber's legal management server, or for accessing public judicial listings (eCourts). No (Only connects directly to configured servers)
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Detects internet availability to automatically switch to offline demonstration/fallback mode when disconnected. No (Processed purely on-device)

No Sensitive Permissions: NERI does not request or access sensitive device features such as Camera, Microphone, GPS/Fine Location, Address Book/Contacts, Call Logs, SMS, or External Shared Storage.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, lease, trade, or transfer personal identification information or case records to any external third party for commercial, advertising, marketing, or behavioral tracking purposes.

Public Judicial Services: When you utilize eCourts features (such as 16-character CNR lookups), the query retrieves publicly available court status directly from public records. No personal identification details or user identity information are transmitted during these requests.

5. Data Security

We employ industry-standard security practices to ensure application integrity:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS): All client-server communications use modern TLS encryption to safeguard data in transit against interception.
  • Code Hardening: The application binary is compiled and protected with R8 minification to prevent tampering and ensure binary integrity.
  • App Sandbox Isolation: All sensitive local cache is confined to Android's secure application sandbox, inaccessible to other apps installed on the device.

6. User Data Control and Deletion

Because we do not store your data on external developer servers, you retain full ownership and control over your records at all times:

  • Clear App Data: You can clear all cached files and local configurations at any time via Android System Settings > Apps > NERI > Storage > Clear Storage.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application automatically and permanently deletes all locally stored data, cache, and preferences associated with NERI from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are strictly oriented towards legal practitioners, law students, and chamber administrators. We do not knowingly solicit or collect information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable jurisdictions).

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect application enhancements or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
